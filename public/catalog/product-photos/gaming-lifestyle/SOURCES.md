## Earlier sources

Local product photography for the iJoy catalogue. The files were collected on
2026-09-07 from official product pages where available, with clean product
shots used when an official page did not expose a downloadable catalogue
image. Keeping copies here prevents product cards from
depending on a third-party CDN at runtime.
